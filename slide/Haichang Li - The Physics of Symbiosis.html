The Shift

My transition from designing interfaces to architecting intelligence.

With a 0-to-1 background spanning AI Startups, Research, PM, and VC, I focus on the gaps between Research, Product, and Market. Previously, my work received the CES AI Best Innovation Award and Purdue DUIRI Fellowship, with features in Yahoo! Tech and Tech Xplore.

My early training was rooted in Human–AI Interaction. Initially, when model agency was limited, I focused on making AI an "Iron Man suit" for humans—using agentic systems to understand user intent for tasks like MV generation and 3D modeling.

However, I have moved beyond surface-level Interaction. I realized that constraining AI within fixed UI pipelines limits its potential. I resonate with The Bitter Lesson — "We want AI agents that can discover like we can, not which contain what we have discovered".

The Goal: To grow AI from a supportive "suit" into a true "Iron Man"—an autonomous entity capable of understanding personalized intent and context, driving the Human-AI relationship from the bottom up.
